4 Gameplay, Minigames & Offline Access

Duo Clash offers a curated collection of 47 local two-player head-to-head duels and solo casual arcade minigames. Key aspects of the service include:

  • Offline Playability: All minigames are designed to run completely offline on a single device without requiring an active internet connection.
  • Zero Mandatory Accounts: No account creation, username registration, or password setup is required to play the games.
  • Local Save Storage: Your game progression, star unlocks, and settings are saved locally in private app storage. Clearing your device app data or uninstalling the game will remove your locally stored records unless backed up via optional Google services.

5 Virtual Items, Boosters & In-Game Hearts

Duo Clash incorporates virtual mechanics such as Hearts, continues, revives, and stage boosters (e.g., hints and shuffles):

  • No Monetary Value: Hearts, continues, and boosters are purely virtual gameplay tokens. They hold zero monetary, cash, or real-world value.
  • Non-Transferable & Non-Refundable: Virtual items cannot be redeemed, sold, exchanged, transferred, or refunded for real currency or merchandise under any circumstances.
  • Free Refills: Playing, retrying, and advancing stages is always 100% free and unlimited. Hearts are used strictly for optional in-game boosters and defeat revives, which can be refilled via daily timers or by voluntarily watching Google AdMob rewarded videos.
